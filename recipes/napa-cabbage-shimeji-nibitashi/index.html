Napa Cabbage & Shimeji Nibitashi

Serves 2Total ~15 minutesOne pot
Home

Ingredients

Warm, brothy side dish
  • 2 large napa cabbage leaves
  • 1/2 package shimeji mushrooms (about 50–60 g)
  • 150 ml dashi
  • 1 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin

Steps

  1. Prepare the vegetables. Cut the cabbage into 3–4 cm pieces, keeping the firm white stems separate from the leafy parts. Trim the shimeji base and separate the mushrooms.
  2. Bring the broth to a simmer. Combine the dashi, soy sauce, and mirin in a small saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat.
  3. Start the firm pieces. Add the shimeji and cabbage stems. Simmer for 2–3 minutes, stirring once so everything cooks evenly.
  4. Add the leaves. Add the cabbage leaves, cover loosely, and simmer until all the cabbage is tender and slightly collapsed, about 3–4 minutes.
  5. Rest briefly. Turn off the heat and let the vegetables sit in the broth for 2 minutes so the flavours settle.
  6. Serve warm. Divide the cabbage and mushrooms between small bowls and spoon a little cooking liquid over each portion.

Adapted from Hitoketa’s shimeji and napa cabbage nibitashi on Rakuten Recipe. The stems and leaves are staggered slightly for even cooking.
