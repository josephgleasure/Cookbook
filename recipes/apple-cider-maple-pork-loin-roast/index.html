Apple Cider & Maple Pork Loin Roast

Serves 10–12Roast ~65–90 minFinish at 145°F / 63°C
Home
Version:

Christmas dinner: 2.5 kg tied boneless loin, planned for 10–12 servings. This version is not yet finalized by testing.

Ingredients

Christmas dinner · 2.5 kg
  • 2.5 kg boneless pork loin, tied
  • 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 4 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 75 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 3 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 250 ml dry apple cider
  • Olive oil, for searing
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
Thanksgiving test · 1.1 kg
  • 1.1 kg boneless pork loin, sirloin half
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 35 g unsalted butter, softened
  • 1 tbsp + 1 tsp pure maple syrup
  • 150 ml dry apple cider
  • Olive oil, for searing
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Practical, untested adaptation for a less-uniform sirloin-half roast. Tie it compactly, begin checking early, and let the thermometer—not the clock—decide.

Steps

  1. Take the chill off. Remove the roast from the refrigerator about 30 minutes before cooking. Pat it dry, tie if needed for an even shape, and season generously with salt and black pepper.
  2. Heat the oven. Preheat to 350°F / 175°C with a rack near the centre.
  3. Sear every side. Heat a little olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown the pork for about 2–3 minutes per side, then transfer it to an oven-safe roasting vessel.
  4. Add herbs, butter, and maple. Strip and roughly chop the rosemary and thyme. Press them onto the pork, spread the softened butter over the surface, and brush with the deliberately restrained maple syrup.
  5. Add cider and roast uncovered. Pour the dry cider around—not over—the pork. Roast uncovered, occasionally spooning pan juices over the meat. Begin checking around 60 minutes; expect roughly 65–90 minutes.Begin checking around 30 minutes; expect roughly 35–55 minutes.
  6. Use the thermometer. Probe the thickest part. The pork must reach 145°F / 63°C after resting. The large roast may be removed somewhat below 145°F only if carryover is actively monitored and the final temperature is verified.Do not assume this smaller, irregular roast will gain 10°F; expect less carryover and check it during the rest.
  7. Rest loosely covered. Rest 15–20 minutes.Rest 10–15 minutes. Confirm the centre reaches 145°F / 63°C before slicing.
  8. Finish and serve. Remove the twine and slice. Spoon the cider-maple roasting juices over the pork. If the juices need more body, simmer them briefly in a saucepan until lightly concentrated.

Adapted from Le Creuset Canada’s Pork Roast with Apple Cider, Maple Syrup & Brussels Sprouts. Recipe image © Le Creuset Canada. The vegetables are omitted and the maple is intentionally reduced.
