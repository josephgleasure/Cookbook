Garlic Butter Glazed Carrots & Green Beans

Serves 2Prep 10 minutesCook 15–20 minutesStovetop
Home
1× recipe

Ingredients

Finish just before serving
  • 227 g carrots, peeled and cut on the diagonal into 5 mm-thick pieces
  • 113 g green beans, trimmed and halved crosswise
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 0.25 tsp fine salt
  • A small pinch of black pepper
  • 14 g unsalted butter
  • 2.5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Finely grated Parmesan and lemon zest, to finish (optional)

Steps

  1. Cut the carrots evenly and halve the green beans. Heat a wide skillet with a lid over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the olive oil and carrots. Cook for 3 minutes, stirring once or twice, until the cut sides begin to colour.
  3. Add the green beans, honey, salt, pepper, and a small splash of water. Cover and cook for 4 minutes, shaking the pan once, until crisp-tender.
  4. Uncover and cook for 2 to 4 minutes, tossing often, until the water evaporates, the carrots are tender, and both vegetables have browned spots. Transfer them to a warm plate.
  5. Lower the heat. Add the butter and sliced garlic to the empty skillet. Cook gently for 3 to 5 minutes, swirling often, until the butter smells nutty, brown flecks appear, and the garlic is golden—not dark brown.
  6. Remove the skillet from the heat and stir in the lemon juice. Return the vegetables and toss through the garlic brown butter. Finish with Parmesan and lemon zest if using, then serve immediately.

Adapted for the stovetop from Bites with Bri’s Roasted Green Beans and Carrots. The 1× setting is one-third of the source recipe and serves two; every measured ingredient scales together with the selected serving count.
