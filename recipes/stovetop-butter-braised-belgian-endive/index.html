Stovetop Butter-Braised Belgian Endive

Serves 25–6 endivesStovetop onlySource-faithful

Source fidelity: quantities and stovetop method follow Molly Watson’s published recipe. This page only scales the original 4-serving recipe to 2 or 8.

Ingredients

  • 5–6 Belgian endives
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • ½ tbsp lemon juice
  • ⅛ tsp sea salt
  • 40 ml water, chicken stock, or white wine
  • ½ tsp sugar, optional

Steps

  1. Heat a large, heavy sauté pan or pot with a tight-fitting lid over medium-low heat and add the butter.
  2. Trim browned stem ends and remove bruised exterior leaves, keeping each endive intact. Arrange the endives in a single layer.
  3. Sprinkle with lemon juice and sea salt. Pour the water, chicken stock, or white wine down the side of the pan. Add the optional sugar if you want to further soften the bitterness.
  4. Cover tightly, reduce the heat to low, and cook undisturbed for about 30 minutes, until very tender.
  5. Remove the lid and cook over medium heat until the liquid evaporates and the endives begin to colour.
  6. Turn the endives and continue cooking until browned all over. Watch carefully if sugar was added.
  7. Serve hot or warm.
