Stovetop Butter-Braised Belgian Endive

Serves 7–8 as a side6–8 endivesStovetop onlyMake ahead
Home

Christmas make-ahead test: stop after the covered braise. Cool and refrigerate the endives in a little braising liquid, then reheat and perform the uncovered reduction and caramelization shortly before dinner.

Ingredients

  • 6 to 8 Belgian endives
  • About 1½ tbsp unsalted butter
  • About ¼ cup / 60 ml chicken stock
  • About 2 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt, to taste
  • Up to ½ tsp sugar, optional

Steps

  1. Trim only the very bottom of each endive so the heads remain intact. Remove any damaged outer leaves.
  2. Melt the butter in a wide sauté pan with a lid over medium-low heat. Arrange the endives in a single layer.
  3. Add the chicken stock, lemon juice, and a pinch of salt. Add the sugar only if you want to temper the endives’ bitterness. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
  4. Cover, turn the heat to low, and braise for 25 to 30 minutes, turning occasionally, until the endives are completely tender.
  5. For make-ahead, stop here. Cool promptly with a little braising liquid, cover, and refrigerate for up to 2 days. Reheat gently in the same wide pan before continuing.
  6. Remove the lid and increase the heat moderately. Let the remaining liquid reduce around the endives, turning them in the buttery glaze until they develop light golden caramelization.
  7. Taste for salt and acidity. Serve hot or warm.
