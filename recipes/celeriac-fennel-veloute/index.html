Celeriac & Fennel Velouté

Serves 8 small portions3:1 celeriac to fennelNo creamMake ahead
Home

Christmas make-ahead: make the soup completely the day before and refrigerate promptly. Reheat gently, loosen with a little stock if it thickens overnight, and make the final lemon adjustment only after reheating. The November test is checking the 3:1 ratio and whether the soup stays silky overnight.

Ingredients

  • About 750 g celeriac, peeled and cut into even cubes
  • About 250 g fennel bulb, trimmed and sliced; reserve fronds if desired
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 30 g unsalted butter
  • About 1 litre chicken stock, kept hot
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and white pepper, to taste
  • 1 to 2 tsp lemon juice
  • 15 to 20 g cold butter, optional, to finish

Steps

  1. Prepare all the vegetables. Melt 30 g butter over medium-low heat in a large saucepan.
  2. Add the onion and fennel with a small pinch of salt. Sweat gently until soft and translucent without developing much colour, about 8 to 10 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for about 1 minute. Add the celeriac, thyme, and bay leaf.
  4. Add enough hot chicken stock to comfortably cover the vegetables; you may not need the full litre yet. Bring to a simmer, cover partially, and cook for 20 to 30 minutes, until the celeriac is completely tender.
  5. Remove the thyme stems and bay leaf. Blend extremely thoroughly until completely smooth, taking care with the hot liquid. Add more hot stock as needed to reach a light, silky soup consistency.
  6. Season with salt and white pepper. Add 1 tsp lemon juice, taste, and add more only if needed—the soup should taste fresh, not lemony. Blend or whisk in the cold butter now if serving immediately.
  7. To make ahead, cool promptly in shallow containers, cover, and refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat gently until steaming, adjust the consistency with stock, then correct the lemon and seasoning. Add the optional cold butter just before serving.
