Celeriac & Fennel Soup

Serves 4

Source fidelity: the ingredient ratio and method below follow Simple Seasonal’s published recipe. This page only scales that recipe to 4 or 8 servings.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups diced white onion
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1½ cups (~130 g) chopped fennel
  • 4½ cups (~700 g) chopped celeriac
  • 7 cups low-sodium vegetable stock
  • Salt and white pepper, to taste
  • 1 cup curly parsley, plus more for garnish
  • 4 tbsp crème fraîche
  • 4 tsp olive oil

Steps

  1. Prepare all the vegetables. Melt the butter in a large saucepan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion, fennel, and celeriac. Cook for about 3 minutes, then add white pepper and cook for 1 minute more.
  3. Add the low-sodium vegetable stock and bring to a boil. Reduce to medium-low and simmer for about 45 minutes, until the celeriac is beginning to fall apart and the fennel is tender.
  4. Purée until smooth, working in batches if using a countertop blender and taking care with the hot liquid.
  5. Blend in the parsley until it is broken into small pieces but not completely puréed into the soup. Season with salt and additional white pepper.
  6. Serve hot, dividing the crème fraîche and olive oil among the bowls and garnishing with parsley.
