Garlic Butter Glazed Carrots & Broccoli

Serves 6Prep 15 minutesCook 20 minutesStovetop
Home

Ingredients

Finish just before serving
  • 700 g carrots, peeled and cut on the diagonal into 1 cm-thick pieces
  • 600 g broccoli (about 2 small heads), stems peeled and sliced, crowns cut into bite-size florets
  • 4 tbsp (55 g) unsalted butter, divided
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1½ tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, plus finely grated zest of ½ lemon
  • ¾ tsp fine salt, plus more for the cooking water
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley (optional)

Steps

  1. Bring a large, wide pot of well-salted water to a boil. Meanwhile, cut the carrots evenly. Peel any tough broccoli stems and slice them before separating the florets.
  2. Add the carrots to the boiling water and cook for 5 minutes. Add the sliced broccoli stems and cook for 1 minute more.
  3. Add the broccoli florets and cook for 2 minutes, just until vivid green and barely tender. Drain everything very well; let the steam escape for 2 minutes so the glaze will cling instead of turning watery.
  4. Return the dry pot to medium heat. Melt 3 tbsp butter, add the garlic, and cook for 30 to 45 seconds until fragrant but not browned. Stir in the honey, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  5. Add the drained vegetables. Toss gently over medium-high heat for 2 to 3 minutes, until glossy and the carrots are tender with a little bite. Fold in the remaining 1 tbsp butter and the lemon zest.
  6. Taste and adjust the salt, pepper, or lemon. Transfer to a warm serving dish, scatter with parsley, and serve immediately. For advance prep, blanch and drain the vegetables up to 4 hours ahead; refrigerate once cool, then make the glaze and finish them just before dinner.

A broccoli variation of the Christmas-menu garlic butter glazed carrots and green beans. Broccoli is added last so it stays bright and does not overcook.
